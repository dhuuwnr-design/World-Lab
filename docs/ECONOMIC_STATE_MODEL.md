# Household Economic State

WORLD LAB needs economic circumstances before individual decisions can become meaningful. A person's choices should eventually depend on household resources, income, prices, employment, obligations and expectations.

This first layer is deliberately narrow. It models annual household cash flow from existing member income, basic consumption, housing costs and residual saving. It does not claim to model a complete economy.

## Current mechanism

- member incomes are summed;
- adult and child counts determine provisional basic costs;
- housing cost is a share of household income;
- remaining cash contributes to household money;
- a small precautionary-saving term is applied;
- household conditions update an individual's provisional status-security signal.

All coefficients are provisional and must be calibrated by geography, time period and household characteristics before realistic claims.

## Required next layers

1. employment transitions and unemployment duration;
2. wages and occupation-specific earnings;
3. household consumption categories;
4. wealth, debt and credit;
5. prices and inflation;
6. firms and production;
7. taxes, transfers and public services;
8. housing supply and rents;
9. inequality and mobility;
10. calibration against household survey and administrative data.

The long-term causal loop is:

work -> income -> consumption/saving -> demand -> firms -> jobs/wages -> household choices -> education/migration/technology adoption -> economy.

Economic state must remain an input to decisions, not a hidden personality coefficient.
