# Cognition Evolution Path

WORLD LAB's cognition system must grow from a reproducible mechanism into richer artificial agency without pretending to reproduce consciousness.

## Layers

1. **Bounded perception** — agents only receive observations available to them.
2. **Memory** — experiences persist and influence later decisions.
3. **Beliefs** — agents maintain uncertain internal models and update them from evidence.
4. **Goals, needs and values** — decisions depend on heterogeneous priorities.
5. **Planning** — agents evaluate multi-step consequences under limited horizons.
6. **Learning** — outcomes change future strategies.
7. **Social cognition** — agents infer intentions, reputation, norms and expectations from interaction.
8. **Communication** — information moves through people and institutions rather than appearing globally.
9. **Culture and transmission** — beliefs and practices can spread, mutate and disappear.
10. **Emergent collective cognition** — institutions and groups develop persistent knowledge and strategies.

## Scientific constraint

Every layer needs empirical targets before it is described as realistic. A richer mechanism is not automatically a more accurate mechanism.

We should prefer simple models that reproduce observed patterns over elaborate models that merely produce convincing stories.

## Emergence requirement

The engine must never encode every interesting macro outcome as a scripted rule. Macro patterns should increasingly arise from interactions among agents, institutions and the physical world.

## Evaluation

Each cognition milestone should be tested for:

- behavioural realism against a defined dataset or study
- heterogeneity across people
- sensitivity to information availability
- learning over time
- reproducibility under fixed seeds
- robustness across seeds
- computational scaling
- failure modes and uncertainty
