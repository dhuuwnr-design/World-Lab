# WORLD LAB social, cultural and affective model

Culture is context, not stereotype. A country is not assigned one personality. WORLD LAB represents a culture profile, individual variation, relationships and institutions, life history, and eventually changing social norms.

Dimensions include family salience, achievement/status salience, perceived respect, belonging, stress, loneliness, wellbeing, norm sensitivity and emotional states. These are model variables, not claims that a country has a fixed emotional profile.

The engine can represent concerns such as family approval, social respect or career prestige when an evidence-backed profile supplies those tendencies, while preserving large individual variation. No country-specific value is treated as factual merely because it is coded. Mature profiles must carry source, year, geography, population scope, uncertainty and calibration method.

The current coefficients are explicitly provisional. Future work must add social networks, peer reference groups, norm enforcement, memory, identity, major life events, media exposure, intergenerational transmission and calibrated behavioural choice models.
